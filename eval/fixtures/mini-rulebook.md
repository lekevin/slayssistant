# Slay the Spire: The Board Game

## Combat

Combat resolves in rounds. Each round every player takes a turn, then enemies act.

### Energy

Each player begins their turn with 3 Energy. Unspent Energy is lost at the end of the turn.

<!-- page: 2 -->

### Blocking

Block reduces incoming damage. Block is lost at the start of your next turn unless a card says otherwise.

#### Timing

A player may only play Block cards during their own turn.

### Exhaust

When a card is Exhausted, remove it from play for the rest of the combat. Exhausted cards return to your deck when combat ends.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## Rewards

After defeating enemies, each player draws one reward card.
