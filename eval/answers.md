## False-permission rate

> **5.6%** — 4 of 71 questions where the rulebook does not grant permission were answered as though it does. 95% CI 2–14%.

This is the number that reflects whether the system is trustworthy. Retrieval recall cannot see it: a pipeline can retrieve the right section and still invent a permission it does not contain.

### Why the `permitted` row is here

A system that answered "the rules don't say" to everything would post a 0% false-permission rate and be worthless. The guard against buying a clean headline with refusals is the permitted stratum: **43/48** correctly answered as allowed, with **5** wrongly reported as unsettled. Read the two numbers together or neither means anything.
